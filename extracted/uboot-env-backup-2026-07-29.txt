## Restoring Android boot

The USB boot change only replaces `bootcmd`, and saves the original to
`bootcmd_android` first. To put the box back the way it was:

    setenv bootcmd "${bootcmd_android}"
    saveenv

If `bootcmd_android` is ever lost, paste the `bootcmd` line above verbatim.
